Research on recycling and utilization of campus paper waste based on
second-hand book trading platform
LIN Mengyao, WU Guanghua, TIAN Shuang, WANG Ping, LI Xiaoya
（
Harbin University of Commerce, Harbin 150028, China
）
Abstract:
With the rapid development of modern economy, paper waste classification is becoming more and more
important in daily life. On university campuses, most recyclable paper waste is not reused. In view of this situation,
paper garbage recycling bins are placed on campus, and an official account platform for second-hand book trading is
created to popularize the relevant knowledge of paper garbage classification online and offline. The complete offline
recycling system and the public account operation complement each other, promoting the efficient recycling and
utilization of campus paper waste.
